Sky Calculator

About

This is a currency calculator for the game Sky:Children of the Light. It is intended to help calculate how many candles you must acquire in a day to meet a stated goal in a certain amount of days.

Candle Calculator

How to Use

Number of Heart Trades: If you regularly send hearts daily, input the amount of hearts you send each day. (The calculation will account for the total amount spent on hearts to your total goal.)

Days left until Deadline: Input the amount of days you have to reach your goal.

Candle Goal: Input the total amount of candles you wish to have. (This will be the total amount of candles you have by the deadline, not how many you wish to collect in the time period.)

Current amount of Candles: Input the total amount of candles you currently have.
